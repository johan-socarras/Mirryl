Good to know
  • Click a phone on the left to open it in a tab; click it again to come back to that tab.
  • ⧉ on a tab moves that mirror to a window of its own, without reconnecting. ⇲ brings it back.
  • In a mirror, Right-click is Back and Middle-click is Home. More shortcuts in Settings.
Setup Assistant

Pair with the phone

Choose whichever is faster for you - both work the same way.
Step 1 of 2
First time? Turn on Wireless debugging on the phone
  1. Turn on Developer options: go to Settings > About phone and tap Build number 7 times. On some phones it is under Settings > About phone > Software information.
  2. Go to Settings > System > Developer options (on Samsung, Settings > Developer options) > Wireless debugging, and turn it on.
  3. Keep the phone on the same Wi-Fi network as this computer, then pair with one of the two options below.
Scan QR code

Open Wireless debugging > Pair device with QR code on the phone, then point the camera at this code.

Optional: silence the "Wireless debugging connected" notification

Android shows this notification every time Wireless debugging turns on. On the phone, long-press it once, tap the gear icon, and turn off sound and vibration for that channel. It stays quietly in the notification shade - Android does not allow hiding it completely - but it no longer pops up or makes noise. The setting survives reboots.
